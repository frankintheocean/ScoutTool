👀 Category watchlists

Follow a Twitch category to spot new or trending channels streaming it, without having to already know their name. Click "Check now" any time to see who's live — new channels since your last check are flagged.

🗺️ Raid map

Tracked streamers grouped by region, coarsely resolved from each streamer's on-file timezone — not a precise geocode, but enough to spot where your roster is concentrated (and where it isn't) at a glance.

🔔 Notification / alert rules

Get notified when a tracked streamer goes live, or when a Discover search finds a match. Choose one or more delivery channels per rule — Browser shows up in the live feed on the left, Webhook POSTs JSON to a URL you provide, and Email needs SMTP configured in .env (see README).
